A person who knows Python, or an agent. Plain declarative Mojo: view() describes the screen, update() answers events. No Swift, Kotlin, JavaScript or shader language.

⚡ The GPU engine

Group-bys, pivots, filters and formulas run as kernels: under a millisecond on 20M rows today; NVIDIA, Apple and phone GPUs when it's done. Same language as the buttons.

🗄️ Data in place

MX frame columns shared by every window and tab, edits as a tiny overlay, Parquet in and out, AI column types.

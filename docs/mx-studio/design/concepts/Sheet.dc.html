What Excel can't do here
No 1,048,576-row wall. The sheet is the ledger, 2.4B rows deep.
Formulas are column-wide. Write it once in words; it runs on every row on the GPU.
Every header is a histogram. Outliers show before you look for them.
Cells hold views. A chart, pivot or what-if is just a cell that recalcs.
It never goes stale. New transactions stream in; everything downstream updates.

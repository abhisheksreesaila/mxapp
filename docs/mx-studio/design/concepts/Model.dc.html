Measure · Margin after freight
1 · You wrote
Revenue minus product cost minus freight, as a share of revenue. Leave out returned orders. Freight comes from the rate table that was active on the order date.
2 · Compiled to Mojo · runs on GPU
fn margin_after_freight(t: Txns) -> Pct:
  let ok = t.where(not t.returned)
  let fr = ok.asof_join(rate_tables,
           on=t.carrier, at=t.date).freight
  return (ok.revenue - ok.cost - fr).sum()
         / ok.revenue.sum()

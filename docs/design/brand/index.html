omxplorer · logo and colour

Eight logo concepts (numbers 1–8) and four accent colours (letters A–D), each colour shown on the real Sheet and Report screens. Reply with something like "logo 1 + colour C", or mix: "logo 5's idea, logo 8's pixels". Everything here is Liquid light, Inter, the same layout; only the accent and the mark change.

Today: teal #0e7c86, no mark, and the active button is a grey-teal wash. White on it is 4.95:1 (passes), but teal text on its own wash is 4.33:1 (fails AA), which is part of why the active "Format" button looks muddy. Every direction below makes the active button a solid accent pill with white text and keeps accent text above 4.5:1 on its wash.

4 · Recommendation

Logo 1 (Lens · bars) + colour C (Iris indigo, #4f46e5)

Why logo 1: it is the idea you described, a magnifying glass as the o, showing data, and it is the one that still reads at 16 px: a ring, a handle and three bars are three shapes, nothing finer. It also works as the o in the wordmark. Cons: it is the most literal; a lens over a bar chart is a common "analytics" icon (Stitch drew a stock one), so on its own it is not very ownable. If you want something only omxplorer could have, logo 5 (the selection ring with the fill handle) is the cleverer runner-up, at the price of reading less instantly as a magnifier.

Why colour C: in a spreadsheet, green and red are already taken: they mean true/false, up/down, good/error. A green accent (A) blends into every "true" pill and into Excel; a red one (B) makes the selection ring look like an error and forces the "false" pills grey. Indigo leaves both free, has the most contrast headroom of the four (6.3:1 white on accent, 5.3:1 on its own wash) and looks distinct from Numbers, Sheets and Excel. Cons: it is the house colour of many SaaS tools, so it is less ownable than red; the formula-value purple has to move to cyan; and it is not the green or red you leaned towards. If being recognised as "a spreadsheet" matters more than the yes/no colours, A is the honest second choice.

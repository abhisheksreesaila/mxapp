Why this isn't Excel

  • No chart-type gallery dialog and no ribbon "Chart Design" tab: the toolbar only inserts.
  • You never pick a type blind: every variant is a live thumbnail of your data.
  • Data is a query (combine, group, sort, top N, filter) run on the GPU, not a cell range you re-select.
  • The chart's own words are its settings (direction 3): the title is the spec.

Motion used

  • One curve, cubic-bezier(0.22,1,0.36,1), no bounce.
  • Bars move to new heights and places in 320 ms; a type change cross-fades (150 ms out, 220 ms in).
  • Sub-inspector pages slide 280 ms (the parent page drifts 30 % left and fades).
  • Segmented thumbs and toggles slide 220 ms; hovers 150 ms.

Data in this prototype

  • 4,000 synthetic orders across 2025: amount, region (North, South, East, West, Central), day, paid.
  • Every change recomputes the groups for real (sum, average, count, median, top N, period, paid).
  • Colours follow the entity, never its rank: West keeps its colour when sorting or filtering.
